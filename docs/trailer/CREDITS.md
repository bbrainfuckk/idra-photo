# Idra Photo — Keep the look

Created 2026-10-16 for [Idra Photo](https://github.com/bbrainfuckk/idra-photo).

36 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 16:9. Caption-led, with instrumental music and sound effects; no spoken narration or voice provider was used.

## Picture and content

Original scene design, typography, timing, and workflow illustrations authored for this trailer. The product photographs, accepted dashboard/settings concepts, and rejected dashboard are the actual Codex `image_gen` results published in `docs/results` on 2026-10-16. Their original prompts, hashes, and review evidence remain in the repository. No new images or third-party stock footage were added.

The prompt, brief, review, and client-connection graphics illustrate the real workflow. The short request shown is illustrative; exact generation prompts remain in the results manifests. They are not a recording of a separate Idra graphical application. Visual review judgments come from the host; the trailer does not claim guaranteed pixel-perfect output or a completed autonomous Claude generation test. Claude requires a connected image-generation tool.

## Sound

Instrumental music is synthesized by the scene with idra-motion's sound API. The click, tick, pop, whoosh, and impact effects use the bundled idra-motion sound kit, identified by that project as public domain / CC0. No commercial song, recorded performer, or cloned voice is used.

The MP4 soundtrack is mixed to a target of -16 LUFS by the idra-motion renderer. Technical measurements are recorded separately in `verification.json`.

## Rendering and source

Rendered with [idra-motion](https://github.com/bbrainfuckk/idra-motion), headless Chromium, and FFmpeg. idra-motion is source available under its own license; its engine, fonts, sound files, and executables are not redistributed here. The custom scene imports its installed engine API. The resulting videos and original scene are for the Idra Photo project.

Deliverables: a 60 fps master with motion blur, a 30 fps web cut, and a poster. The repository includes both video versions, the poster, and the original scene.
